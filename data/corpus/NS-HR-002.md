# Employee Relations Investigations

Restricted to the HR group.

## Starting an investigation

An HR business partner must open an investigation within 5 business days of receiving a formal complaint.

## Choosing an investigator

The investigator must be outside the reporting line of everyone involved. Complaints involving a VP or above are investigated by external counsel.

## Case records

Investigation files are stored in the ER-Vault folder of the HR information system, which only HR can access.
