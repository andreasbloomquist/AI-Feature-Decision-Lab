# Procurement and Purchase Approvals

This policy covers purchases of goods, software and services from vendors. Travel and personal expenses follow the Travel Policy and the Expense Reimbursement Policy instead.

## Approval thresholds

Purchases under $5,000 need approval from your manager. Purchases from $5,000 to $25,000 need approval from your department head and from Finance. Purchases over $25,000 need CFO approval and at least 3 competitive quotes.

## Software

Any new software or SaaS purchase, whatever its cost, needs a Security review before the contract is signed.

## Contract signatures

Only authorized signatories may sign vendor contracts. The VP of Finance may sign contracts up to $50,000. Contracts over $50,000 and all multi-year commitments must be signed by the CFO or CEO.

## Vendor gifts

Employees may not accept gifts from vendors worth more than $75 (UK: £60). Report any gift above this value to Procurement.
