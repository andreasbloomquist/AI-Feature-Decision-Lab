# Travel Policy (2026)

This policy covers business travel for all Northstar employees in the US and UK. It replaces the Travel Policy (2025), NS-TRV-2025, from 1 January 2026.

## Booking

All flights, rail and hotels must be booked through TripDesk, Northstar's travel booking tool. Bookings made outside TripDesk are not reimbursed unless TripDesk had no available option and your manager approved the exception in writing. Book at least 14 days before departure when possible.

## Trip approvals

Every trip needs approval before booking, based on the estimated total trip cost. Trips up to $2,000 (UK: £1,600) need approval from your direct manager only. Trips over $2,000 (UK: £1,600) need approval from your department head (VP) in addition to your manager. Trips over $7,500 (UK: £6,000) also need approval from the CFO.

## Flights

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires CFO approval before booking, regardless of flight length.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap in standard US cities is $250. In high-cost US cities (New York, San Francisco, Boston and Seattle) the cap is $325 per night. In the UK the cap is £180 per night, or £240 per night in London. Caps exclude taxes.

## Meals

Travelers receive a meal per diem of $75 per day in the US and £60 per day in the UK. The per diem covers meals and incidental tips. Client meals are covered by the Client Entertainment and Gifts policy, not the per diem.

## Ground transport

Rideshare, taxis and public transport are reimbursable. Personal car use is reimbursed at the IRS standard mileage rate in the US and 45p per mile in the UK.

## Changes from the 2025 policy

The 2026 policy made these changes to the 2025 policy:

- The manager-only approval threshold rose from $1,500 to $2,000 (UK: from £1,200 to £1,600).
- Premium economy is now allowed on flights of 6 hours or longer; previously it required 8 hours or longer.
- The US meal per diem rose from $65 to $75 per day.
- The standard US hotel cap rose from $225 to $250 per night.
- Booking through TripDesk is now required; previously any booking site was allowed if receipts were submitted.
