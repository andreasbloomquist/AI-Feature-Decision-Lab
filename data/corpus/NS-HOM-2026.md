# Home Office Equipment Policy (2026)

This policy describes allowances for equipping a home workspace. Work arrangements (fully remote and hybrid) are defined in the Remote and Hybrid Work policy. It replaces the Home Office Equipment Policy (2024), NS-HOM-2024, from 1 March 2026.

## Setup allowance

Fully remote employees receive a one-time setup allowance of $1,000 (UK: £800) for a desk, chair, monitor and accessories. Hybrid employees receive a one-time setup allowance of $500 (UK: £400). No separate approval is needed for purchases within the allowance.

## Annual refresh

After their first work anniversary, fully remote and hybrid employees receive a refresh allowance of $300 (UK: £240) every 12 months for replacing or upgrading equipment. Unused refresh allowance does not carry over.

## Internet stipend

Fully remote employees receive an internet stipend of $50 (UK: £40) per month, paid through payroll. Hybrid employees do not receive an internet stipend.

## How to claim

Submit purchases in Ledgerly with an itemized receipt within 60 days of purchase. Laptops and other computers are provided by IT and cannot be bought with the allowance.

## Eligibility

Contractors, interns on assignments of less than 3 months, and employees assigned to an office full time are not eligible for home office allowances.

## Changes from the 2024 policy

The 2024 policy gave fully remote employees a one-time allowance of $750 (UK: £600) and did not cover hybrid employees. It had no annual refresh allowance and an internet stipend of $40 (UK: £30) per month.
