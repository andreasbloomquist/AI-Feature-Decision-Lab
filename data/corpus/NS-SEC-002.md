# Device and Endpoint Requirements

These requirements apply to every device used to access Northstar systems.

## Company laptops

Northstar laptops are enrolled in FleetGuard, Northstar's device management service. They must have full-disk encryption enabled. Operating system security updates must be installed within 14 days of release. Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.

## Personal devices

Personal phones and tablets may access email and Slack only through approved apps with the FleetGuard mobile profile installed. Customer data must never be stored on a personal device.

## Travel with devices

You may take your Northstar laptop abroad. Outside the US and UK, connect to Northstar systems only through the NorthGate VPN.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device as a security incident, following the Security Incident Reporting policy. IT can lock and wipe enrolled devices remotely.
