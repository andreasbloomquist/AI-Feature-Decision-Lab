# Employee Data Retention

This policy explains how long Northstar keeps records about employees and candidates.

## Personnel files

Personnel files are kept for 7 years after employment ends for US employees and 6 years after employment ends for UK employees. Payroll records follow the same periods.

## Candidate data

Application data for candidates who are not hired is deleted 12 months after the recruiting process ends in the US. In the UK it is deleted 6 months after the process ends, unless the candidate consents to Northstar keeping it for 12 months.

## Workplace communications

Slack messages are retained for 2 years. Email is retained for 3 years. Records under legal hold are kept until Legal releases the hold.

## Access to your own data

Employees can request a copy of their personal data through the People portal. For UK employees, Northstar responds to data subject access requests within one month.
