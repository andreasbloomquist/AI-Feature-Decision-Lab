# Payment Controls and Audit Thresholds

Restricted to the Finance group.

## Expense audit triggers

Finance automatically audits any single expense line over $2,500, and any employee who submits more than 3 Missing Receipt Declarations in a calendar year.

## Vendor bank details

Changes to vendor bank details require a call-back to a phone number already on file before the change is saved.

## Payments

Payment runs happen every Tuesday and Thursday. Wire transfers over $50,000 require approval from two Finance approvers.
