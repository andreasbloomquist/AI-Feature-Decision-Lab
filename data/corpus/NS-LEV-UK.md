# UK Parental Leave Policy

This policy applies only to employees employed by Northstar Ltd in the UK. It enhances statutory leave and pay.

## Eligibility for enhanced pay

Employees qualify for Northstar's enhanced pay after 26 weeks of continuous employment. Employees with less service still receive their statutory entitlements.

## Maternity and adoption leave

Employees taking maternity or adoption leave may take up to 52 weeks of leave. Northstar pays 20 weeks at full pay, followed by statutory pay for the remaining paid weeks.

## Partner leave

Partners of a birth mother or adopter receive 6 weeks of full pay.

## Notice

Tell the People Team at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth, or within 7 days of being matched for adoption.
