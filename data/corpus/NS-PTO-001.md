# Time Off and Holidays

This policy covers vacation and public holidays. Parental leave is covered by separate US and UK policies.

## US employees

US employees have flexible paid time off. Employees are encouraged to take at least 15 days each year. Northstar observes 11 company holidays in the US.

## UK employees

UK employees receive 25 days of annual leave per holiday year, plus the 8 UK bank holidays.

## Carrying over leave in the UK

UK employees may carry up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next holiday year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March.

## Requesting time off

Request time off in the People portal. Give at least 2 weeks' notice for absences of 5 days or more.
