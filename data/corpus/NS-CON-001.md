# Contractor Engagement and Eligibility

This policy covers independent contractors and agency workers engaged by Northstar in any country.

## Engagement

Contractors are engaged through Procurement under a signed statement of work (SOW). An initial engagement may last up to 12 months. Extending an engagement beyond 12 months requires VP approval.

## Benefits eligibility

Contractors are not Northstar employees and are not eligible for employee benefits or allowances. This includes paid parental leave, paid time off, the home office allowances, the learning and development budget and equity.

## Expenses

Contractors may be reimbursed for travel only if their SOW allows it. Contractors claim approved expenses on their invoice, not in Ledgerly.

## Devices and access

Contractors who access Northstar systems must use a Northstar-managed laptop or the approved virtual desktop. Contractors who access customer data must pass a background check first.
