# Security Incident Reporting

Everyone who works for Northstar must report suspected security incidents.

## What to report

Report suspected phishing, malware, unauthorized access, data sent to the wrong recipient, and lost or stolen devices that can access Northstar systems or data.

## How and when to report

Report a suspected incident within 1 hour of discovering it. Post in the #sec-incidents Slack channel or email security@northstar.example. If you cannot reach either, call the Security on-call line listed on the intranet. Use the Report Phish button in your email client for suspected phishing emails.

## What not to do

Do not investigate a suspected incident yourself, forward suspicious attachments, or wipe an affected device unless Security asks you to.

## Response times

Security acknowledges severity 1 incidents within 15 minutes and severity 2 incidents within 4 hours.
