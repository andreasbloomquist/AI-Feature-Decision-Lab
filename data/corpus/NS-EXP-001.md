# Expense Reimbursement Policy

This policy explains how employees claim back business expenses they paid personally.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in Ledgerly, Northstar's expense tool, within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred. For trips, the 30 days start on the last day of the trip. Expense reports are approved by your direct manager. Reports totalling more than $5,000 (UK: £4,000) are also reviewed by Finance Operations.

## Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for every expense of $25 (UK: £20) or more. Card statements alone are not accepted as receipts.

## Missing receipts

If a receipt is lost, you may submit a Missing Receipt Declaration in Ledgerly for an expense of up to $75 (UK: £60). Each employee may use at most 2 Missing Receipt Declarations per calendar quarter. Expenses above these limits without a receipt are not reimbursed. Some expense types, including client entertainment, do not allow Missing Receipt Declarations; see the Client Entertainment and Gifts policy.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved expenses are reimbursed through payroll or bank transfer within 10 business days of approval.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Northstar does not reimburse personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, airline seat upgrades that the Travel Policy does not allow, or alcohol unless it is part of approved client entertainment.
