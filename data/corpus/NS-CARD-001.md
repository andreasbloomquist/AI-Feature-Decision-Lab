# Corporate Card Program

Northstar corporate cards are for business expenses only.

## Eligibility

Employees who travel for work more than 4 times a year, or who are nominated by a department head, may apply for a corporate card through Finance Operations.

## Limits

Cardholders have a monthly limit of $10,000 (UK: £8,000). Temporary increases need approval from Finance Operations.

## Reconciliation

Cardholders must reconcile each card transaction in Ledgerly, with receipts, within 15 days of the statement closing date. Card spend follows the same rules as the Travel Policy and the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## Personal use

Personal purchases on a corporate card are not allowed. Repay any accidental personal charge within 15 days.
