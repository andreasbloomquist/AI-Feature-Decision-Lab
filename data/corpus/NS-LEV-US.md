# US Parental Leave Policy

This policy applies to employees on Northstar Inc.'s US payroll.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for paid parental leave after 90 days of employment. Leave is available for the birth of a child and for adoption or foster placement. Contractors are not covered by this policy.

## Paid leave

Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birth parents, including adoptive and foster parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave.

## Timing

Paid parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. It may be taken in up to 2 separate blocks.

## Notice

Tell the People Team and your manager at least 30 days before your leave starts, or as soon as possible if the timing is unexpected.
