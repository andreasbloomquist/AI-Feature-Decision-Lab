# Learning and Development Budget

Northstar funds courses, certifications, books and conferences that relate to your role.

## Annual budget

Each employee has a learning budget of $1,200 (UK: £1,000) per calendar year. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Approval

Your manager must approve learning spend before you commit to it. Claim approved costs in Ledgerly.

## Conferences

Conference tickets come from the learning budget. Travel to a conference follows the Travel Policy and is paid from your team's travel budget, not the learning budget.

## Eligibility

All employees are eligible from their first day. Contractors are not eligible.
