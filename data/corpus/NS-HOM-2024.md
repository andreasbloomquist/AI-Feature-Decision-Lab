# Home Office Equipment Policy (2024)

Superseded on 1 March 2026 by the Home Office Equipment Policy (2026), NS-HOM-2026. Kept for reference only.

## Setup allowance

Fully remote employees receive a one-time setup allowance of $750 (UK: £600). Hybrid employees are not eligible for a home office allowance.

## Internet stipend

Fully remote employees receive an internet stipend of $40 (UK: £30) per month.

## How to claim

Submit purchases in Ledgerly with an itemized receipt within 60 days of purchase.
