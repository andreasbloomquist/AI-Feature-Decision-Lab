# Travel Policy (2025)

Superseded on 1 January 2026 by the Travel Policy (2026), NS-TRV-2026. Kept for reference only.

## Booking

Employees may book flights and hotels on any booking site, provided itemized receipts are submitted with the expense report.

## Trip approvals

Trips up to $1,500 (UK: £1,200) need approval from your direct manager. Trips over $1,500 (UK: £1,200) need approval from your department head (VP) in addition to your manager. Trips over $7,500 (UK: £6,000) also need approval from the CFO.

## Flights

Book economy class for flights under 8 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 8 hours or longer. Business class requires CFO approval.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap in standard US cities is $225. In high-cost US cities (New York, San Francisco, Boston and Seattle) the cap is $325 per night. In the UK the cap is £180 per night, or £240 per night in London.

## Meals

Travelers receive a meal per diem of $65 per day in the US and £60 per day in the UK.
