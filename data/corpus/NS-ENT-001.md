# Client Entertainment and Gifts

This policy covers meals, events and gifts for clients and prospective clients.

## Spending limits

Client entertainment is limited to $150 (UK: £120) per person, including tax and tip. Any single client event with a total cost over $1,000 (UK: £800) needs prior approval from your department head (VP).

## Documentation

Every client entertainment expense needs an itemized receipt, regardless of amount, and a list of attendees with their names and companies. The Missing Receipt Declaration described in the Expense Reimbursement Policy cannot be used for client entertainment. Client entertainment without an itemized receipt is not reimbursed.

## Gifts to clients

Gifts to clients are limited to $100 (UK: £80) in value per recipient per calendar year. Cash and gift cards may not be given as gifts.

## Government officials

Entertainment or gifts for government officials require prior written approval from Legal, whatever the amount.
