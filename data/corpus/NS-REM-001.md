# Remote and Hybrid Work

This policy defines Northstar's work arrangements.

## Work arrangements

A hybrid employee is assigned to a Northstar office and works there at least 2 days a week. A fully remote employee has no assigned office. An office-based employee works from an assigned office full time. Your arrangement is recorded in your Remote Work Agreement in the People portal.

## Changing your arrangement

Changes to your work arrangement need approval from your manager and the People Team, and an updated Remote Work Agreement.

## Working from another country

Employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with prior approval from the People Team. Longer periods are not permitted.
