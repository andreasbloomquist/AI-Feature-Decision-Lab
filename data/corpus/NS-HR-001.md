# Compensation Bands 2026

Restricted to the HR group. Do not share outside HR.

## Engineering base salary bands

US engineering base salary bands for 2026: Level 4 is $148,000 to $176,000. Level 5 is $182,000 to $218,000. UK engineering Level 5 is £98,000 to £118,000.

## Merit cycle

The 2026 merit increase budget is 3.5% of eligible base payroll. Merit increases take effect on 1 April.

## Exceptions

Offers above the top of a band need approval from the Chief People Officer.
